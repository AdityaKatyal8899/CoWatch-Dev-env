Service Level Agreement

Platform Infrastructure Hosting & Service Availability Agreement

1. Parties & Background

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") is entered into by Aditya Katyal ("Service Provider") as the infrastructure operator and software provider of the CoWatch platform, and the users of the CoWatch application ("Client"). CoWatch provides private virtual watch party environments leveraging AWS compute, storage, and real-time streaming services.

2. Scope of Service & Infrastructure
2.1 Hosting Infrastructure: The Service Provider manages AWS EC2 (Elastic Compute Cloud) hosting, VPC configuration, and S3 (Simple Storage Service) instances to maintain user authentication, media transcode pipelines, and low-latency watch party synchronization.
2.2 Streaming Services: Support is provided for HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) transcode processing and dynamic watch party sync connections.
3. Service Levels & Availability
3.1 Uptime Target: The Service Provider targets a monthly uptime percentage of 99.5% for all primary web services and databases, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.
3.2 Maintenance Windows: System upgrades and optimizations are scheduled during off-peak hours (02:00 AM to 04:00 AM IST) and will be notified to users at least 24 hours in advance.
4. Compliance & Content Ownership
4.1 Intermediary Status: CoWatch acts strictly as an intermediary infrastructure provider. The Service Provider does not review, verify, license, or own the copyrighted video files uploaded by the Client. The Client guarantees they possess the required rights or authorizations for any files streamed within their private, password-secured watch rooms.
4.2 Safe Harbor: Any copyrighted content reported through standard channels will be reviewed and removed immediately in compliance with digital intermediary hosting regulations.
5. Support & Incident Response

Technical support, infrastructure inquiries, or reports of outages will be addressed within 12 to 24 hours. Tickets or inquiries can be submitted via email directly to the Service Provider's registered support channels.
